Table Exporter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Table Exporter is a browser extension that extracts HTML tables from the tab you are viewing and exports them locally, as a CSV file download or as text copied to your clipboard. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with data.

What the extension does

  • Reads the content of an HTML table on the current page only when you click the extension's button in that tab.
  • Turns that table content into a CSV text in your browser's memory.
  • Saves the result on your device: as a CSV file download, or as text placed on your system clipboard — only at your explicit request.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not send, store or share any data with any server. There is no backend, no analytics, no account, and no login.
  • It does not run in the background; it only acts when you invoke it.
  • It does not collect browsing history, personal data, or any data beyond the table you choose to export.
  • It does not modify the web pages you visit.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab and scripting: used strictly to read table content from the active page when you click the extension icon. No background execution.

Your choices

You can remove the extension at any time from your browser's extension settings. Files you download or text you copy stay under your full control, like any other file on your device.

Contact

For questions about this extension, contact the developer at contact@izz.ro or through the Chrome Web Store listing page.
